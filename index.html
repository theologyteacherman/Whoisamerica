Who Is the Nation?

The National Glue Game

In 1963 Dr. King told the country he had a dream. Most people remember it as a dream about being kind to each other. Read it again and you find something harder: a claim about who counts as American, and on what terms. Seven missions, one wall that keeps moving, and a badge at the end if you can say where it lands.
